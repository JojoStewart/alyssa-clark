Approach


Yoga is a tool for meditative, intuitive, mind-body experiences.

As an instructor, my goal is to create a container for students to have an integrated experience, individually and as a community. My teaching focuses on intuitive movement, mindfulness, and mind-body connection, culminating in an extended savasana.

My classes are meditation focused and are accessible for all levels of experience and body types by providing options to accommodate each individual.
